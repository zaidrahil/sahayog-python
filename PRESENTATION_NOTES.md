### Slide 4: Worker Onboarding & Fair Allocation Engine
- **Visuals on Slide**: Two comprehensive cards covering the 4-step Onboarding & Background Verification pipeline and the Anti-Monopoly Fair Allocation Engine.
- **Presenter Script**:
  > *"Slide 4 tackles the two core challenges of trust and allocation fairness.  
  > First, **Onboarding**: Workers cannot simply create an account and start taking jobs. They must pass a 4-tier verification gate: Aadhaar e-KYC, DigiLocker integration with NCCT skill certificates, reference checks from registered cooperative societies, and background verification. A Federation Officer physically or digitally approves the worker profile.  
  > Second, **Fair Work Allocation**: In private gig platforms, top 5% veteran workers capture 60% of jobs due to rating bias, burning themselves out while new workers starve. Sahayog solves this with algorithmic fairness:

  > 2. We introduce a **Cold-Start Boost**: new workers in their first 14 days receive a +25% score boost so they can establish their reputation.  
  > 3. We restrict dispatch to a tight **2 to 3 km hyper-local radius**, ensuring workers don’t waste fuel and unpaid time travelling across cities."*

## 3. Anticipated Jury Questions & Viva Defense Guide

### Q1: "How does the Escrow mechanism actually function if the customer pays via UPI?"
- **Defense Answer**:
  > *"When the customer initiates the booking, our payment gateway adapter creates an escrow mandate via UPI Intent / Razorpay Smart Collect. The money is debited from the customer's account immediately and credited into a nodal cooperative escrow account managed under RBI guidelines. In the database, the transaction is flagged as `status = 'held'`. The worker receives an alert that payment is 100% secured in escrow. Only when the worker enters the customer's completion OTP does the settlement engine invoke the payout API, executing split transfers: 87% to the worker's UPI VPA, 5% to the cooperative welfare wallet, and 8% to the federation account."*

### Q2: "What if a customer refuses to provide the OTP or gives an unfairly low rating just to avoid paying?"
- **Defense Answer**:
  > *"Because funds are already deposited in escrow upfront, the customer cannot cancel payment unilaterally after work has commenced. If a customer disputes the job or withholds the OTP maliciously, the booking enters `status = 'disputed'`. The worker’s uploaded photographic evidence of the completed work, combined with GPS timestamp verification showing the worker was on-site, is reviewed by the Federation Dispute Conciliation Officer within 24 hours. If work is verified, the escrow funds are released automatically."*

### Q3: "How does the 2–3 km allocation algorithm handle rural areas where workers are scattered?"
- **Defense Answer**:
  > *"Our AI allocation engine uses dynamic step-expansion. In high-density urban wards, it enforces a strict 2.0 to 3.0 km radius to minimize travel time and eliminate unpaid travel costs. In rural or semi-urban talukas, if no verified worker is active within 3 km, the algorithm automatically expands the search radius in 1.5 km increments up to a maximum ceiling of 6.0 km, adding a modest union-approved rural travel allowance to the tariff."*

### Q4: "How do you guarantee that newly registered workers are safe to dispatch into homes?"
- **Defense Answer**:
  > *"No worker is dispatched upon self-registration. Our multi-tier onboarding requires four strict checks: (1) Aadhaar e-KYC verifying identity, (2) verified vocational certificates from DigiLocker/NCCT or two authorized reference letters from registered cooperative society members, (3) criminal background self-declaration matched against police verification records, and (4) final sign-off by a Federation Field Officer who inspects their tool kit. Only then is `verified = 1` granted."*

### Q5: "How does your 22-language matching work without exploding translation costs?"
- **Defense Answer**:
  > *"During onboarding, workers select the languages they speak from interactive audio-enabled vernacular chips. This is stored as a simple, high-speed indexed attribute (`spoken_languages`) in the worker profile. When a customer initiates a booking with a preferred language (e.g., Odia or Kannada), the SQL query includes a strict string filter `worker.spoken_languages LIKE '%code%'`. The application interface uses static localized dictionaries for all 22 Eighth Schedule languages, meaning zero per-request translation API costs and instant rendering."*

### Q6: "Why is the 8% federation commission sustainable when corporate apps take 30%?"
- **Defense Answer**:
  > *"Corporate apps spend massive venture capital budgets on user acquisition ads, investor returns, and inflated executive compensations. A cooperative federation is a member-owned non-profit entity governed under the Ministry of Cooperation. An 8% commission on high transaction volumes is more than sufficient to cover cloud server costs, local grievance staff, and cooperative office overhead—leaving the surplus where it belongs: in the pockets of the workers."*
